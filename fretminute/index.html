FretMinute

Ten notes. A place to start.

Choose a small part of the guitar fretboard, answer ten questions, then revisit the notes you missed. FretMinute is a visual touch quiz for standard six-string EADGBE tuning, from open strings to fret 12. It does not listen to your guitar or play sound.

Start a round

Select strings, a start/end fret and a mode. Name the marked note, or find one matching position in the selected range. Note names use A–G; equivalent sharps and flats share an answer. Right- and left-handed views are available.

Each round has ten questions. A small range may repeat notes. Your first answer counts, and Next advances the question. Exiting an unfinished round does not add a completed record.

Read your history

History compares full rounds only when the practice settings match. Different ranges, modes or handedness remain separate. Mistake reviews are separate ten-question rounds and do not change the original result or the full-round history. Scores record answers in the app; they do not measure actual guitar playing or prove learning gains.

Keep a backup you control

Completed practice and preferences are stored on your device. FretMinute has no account or app-operated cloud synchronization. In Settings > Backup & restore, export a JSON file through the system file interface before changing devices or deleting app data.

A restore first previews a valid backup and asks for confirmation. It replaces current local history and preferences; it does not merge two histories. Keep copies you need in a location you control. Files saved to iCloud Drive or another provider are managed under that provider’s settings.

All features free. Tips optional.

All features in this version are free. Small, medium and large tips are optional one-time consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. They are not subscriptions and unlock no content, badges, benefits or features. The app shows the App Store’s current price for your region.

While a purchase is waiting, the prices remain visible, purchase buttons are disabled and the selected option shows a spinner. The Apple purchase sheet may take time to appear. Cancellation, failure or pending approval never locks practice. If tips are unavailable, continue practicing for free. For billing and refund help, use Apple purchase support; do not share Apple Account credentials with the developer.
