Support中文说明

Practice stays on your device

Completed answers, round dates, practice settings and language preferences are stored locally on your device. FretMinute has no account system, advertising, tracking SDK, developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service, or developer-operated practice server. The app does not send your practice records to us. The visual quiz does not record sound or request microphone access.

Files, backups and deletion

You may export a JSON backup containing completed practice records and preferences using the system file interface. You choose where to save it. If you select iCloud Drive or another file provider, that service handles the file under its own settings and privacy terms. FretMinute does not automatically upload backups or provide its own cloud synchronization. iOS device backups may include local app data according to your device settings.

Restoring a valid backup replaces local history and preferences only after preview and confirmation. Deleting history in the app removes local completed records, but does not delete copies you previously exported. Removing the app removes its local app data subject to iOS behavior. Manage exported files and system backups separately.

Optional one-time tips

All features in this version are free. Tips are optional one-time consumable purchases processed by Apple, with no subscription, content or feature entitlement. The app uses StoreKit to display localized prices, observe purchase status and complete verified transactions. It does not request payment card details or send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server. Apple handles Apple Account, payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy. Apple may also provide diagnostics or store reporting according to your Apple settings; this policy does not control Apple’s services.

Support and external links

When you choose Privacy policy or Help & support in the app, the fixed HTTPS page opens in your external browser. FretMinute does not add device identifiers, practice answers, transaction records or contact details to the URL.

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. A backup attachment contains practice records. Send only what is necessary; do not send passwords or payment details. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to records we need to retain for legitimate legal or operational reasons. Retention depends on the purpose of the correspondence and applicable obligations.

This website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or contact form. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes while serving this site, under its own privacy statement. Your browser and any file provider you choose have their own privacy settings.

Your choices

Practice does not require an account or a tip. Exports, file destinations and support messages are your choice. Because practice records are local, we cannot remotely retrieve or erase them. Contact the support email above with privacy questions. We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change; the effective date identifies the current notice.
