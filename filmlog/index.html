FilmLog Support / 使用支持

Keep the story behind every frame. FilmLog is an offline film photography journal for iPhone and iPad.

Using FilmLog

1. Create a roll with your camera, film stock and ISO. 2. Record exposure settings after each shot. 3. Update the status when you send the film to a lab. 4. Link scans to individual frames, or use Pro to match several at once. 5. Export a backup regularly. A purchase restores Pro, not your photo library.

Pro and restoring purchases

The free edition saves three rolls. Pro is an optional one-time Apple In-App Purchase for unlimited rolls and bulk scan matching. Open Settings → Restore purchases to restore a purchase using the same Apple Account. Apple handles payments and refunds. Restoring Pro does not restore your library.

Backups

Export includes all rolls, exposure records and linked photo copies. Save the file in Files or another safe location before reinstalling or changing phones. Restoring replaces your current FilmLog library. Export a backup first if you want to keep it. Purchases and language settings are not changed.
