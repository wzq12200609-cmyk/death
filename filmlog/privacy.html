Privacy policy / 隐私政策

English

FilmLog does not operate a server or collect analytics, advertising identifiers, locations, accounts, rolls or photos. Selected photos are copied to private app storage. Photos are not uploaded by FilmLog. Purchases are handled by Apple; the app checks verified purchase entitlements on your device. Exports are shared only when you choose a destination. Files stored with third-party services or device backups follow those services’ policies. Delete individual rolls or use Clear All Data to remove local content. Uninstalling the app removes its local library. Keep an exported backup if you want to restore it later.

Contact and support requests

You may email wzq12200609@gmail.com for support. If you choose to email us, your message and contact details are processed by the email provider and used to respond to your request. Do not include private photos or backups unless you want to share them for troubleshooting. FilmLog does not automatically send diagnostics or attachments.

如需支持，可发送邮件至 wzq12200609@gmail.com。你主动提供的邮箱、消息及附件由邮件服务商处理，用于回复支持请求。FilmLog 不会自动发送诊断信息或附件。
