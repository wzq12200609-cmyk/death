Privacy policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

StrandStep is a bead-planning and loading tool by Jason Wu. Your plans work on your device without an app account or an app server. This policy explains the app’s data flow and the external services you may choose to use.

Plans and settings stay on your device

The app stores plan names, bead names and colours, bead widths, dimensions, strand sequences, test-swatch details, progress, saved palettes, language settings and the free-project allowance locally. Local identifiers help the app keep these records together; they are not sent to us. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or AI services, and it does not upload plans to us.

Local recovery and deletion

The app keeps the current library, a previous-save recovery copy and a separate free-project record. Deleting a plan removes it from the active library; it may remain in the previous-save copy until a later save replaces that copy. Recovery after damaged storage can also preserve a local copy of the damaged file. These recovery files are not uploaded to us. Deleting a plan does not renew the free-project allowance.

Removing the app removes its app-managed local data under the device’s normal app-removal behaviour. Device backups, exported files and copies saved elsewhere may remain. Manage those copies through the relevant device, file-storage or backup service.

Files and backups you choose

JSON backup, CSV export and sharing save or send files only through the location or app you choose. Files can include your project content. An exported file may be stored in iCloud Drive or another provider if you choose that destination. Apple’s device-backup settings may also include app data. Those services operate under their own terms and policies. Keep a backup before removing the app or replacing local data.

Purchases

Apple processes optional App Store purchases and restores. The app uses StoreKit purchase information to verify whether Full Workshop is available. We do not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase database. Apple processes transaction and account information under its own privacy policy.

Support and website visits

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue, and retain them only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or a complete project library unless it is needed to explain the issue. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Opening support or privacy pages uses your browser and GitHub Pages hosting. Your browser and the hosting service may process ordinary connection data, such as your IP address and request information. The app does not attach plan or purchase data to these links. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies added by us.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact wzq12200609@gmail.com.
