Which tool should I choose?

Single-strand designer: arrange round beads by colour and width along the thread. Enter your target laid-flat length and the exposed clasp/end contribution, repeat or mirror a unit, then check order and quantities before making it.

Eight-strand loading: enter an existing pattern as eight separate loading lists, then follow the next bead on each strand. This is a loading assistant; it does not generate or preview a finished braid pattern.

What do the length and circle views mean?

Single-strand length is measured laid flat, including the exposed clasp and ends. It is not wrist circumference or a guarantee of fit. The circle illustrates colour order, not gemstone rendering or a precise wearing result. Check your physical fit.

Mirror keeps both ends: AAB becomes AABBAA. Fit uses complete repeats only, with no automatic cutting off or dropping beads. The app shows any remaining gap or excess; adjust your unit or dimensions deliberately.

How does an eight-strand test swatch work?

Make a sample using the same beads, cord and braiding method as your project. Enter an equal bead count for each strand and the measured beaded length of the sample. Exposed ends are separate. This produces an advisory estimate; it does not change your eight loading lists. Material, tension and technique affect the result.

Can I pause and continue?

Each strand keeps its next-bead position. Mark a bead when loaded and use Undo if needed. Your saved progress stays with the local plan. Changing the bead order can require resetting affected progress; the app asks before doing so.

What is free, and what does Full Workshop add?

One complete custom plan is free, shared across both tools. Trying the sample does not use it. Deleting or importing does not renew the allowance. JSON backup and CSV export remain free.

Full Workshop is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds more plans, duplication, saved-palette reuse and printable PDF loading sheets. The purchase screen shows your App Store price when available. Existing plans remain accessible if the store cannot load. Limits are 200 plans, 50 saved palettes, 2,000 beads per strand and an 8 MiB JSON library.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the Apple account used for the original purchase. Internet access to the App Store is needed for purchasing or restoring. If it fails, check your account and connection, retry later, then contact support with the app version and error. Do not send your Apple password.

How do I protect or move my plans?

Use JSON backup to keep a restorable library; CSV is useful for reading your lists elsewhere. Save backups in a location you control. Keep the original JSON before importing. Import merges projects by stable project ID: an imported version can update a matching project, while unrelated existing projects remain. Importing a new custom project still uses the shared free allowance. Without Full Workshop, a different new project or a multi-project backup may require the unlock; importing does not bypass the limit or create a fresh allowance. Restoring the already reserved free project is allowed. Review the result before continuing. The app does not run a cloud-sync account. If storage is damaged, preserve the app and backup files and try the offered previous-save recovery before deleting anything.

Most planning and progress work offline. Files stored with external providers, purchase/restore and opening these support pages may require a connection. See the privacy policy for details.
