BurnPair privacy notice

Effective November 1, 2026 · App version 1.0

BurnPair is provided by 志强 武. This notice covers the BurnPair iPhone app and explains how experiment records, photos and purchases are handled. Contact: wzq12200609@gmail.com.

Records stay on your device

Experiment recipes, sample parameters, observations, dates, photos and settings are stored locally on your device. BurnPair has no user account or developer-operated cloud sync. We do not receive your experiment records or photos through the app.

Photos and files you choose

BurnPair opens the system photo picker or camera when you choose to add a photo, and the file picker when you choose a backup. Camera access is requested only when you select that action. Selected photos are resized to a maximum edge of 1600 pixels and re-encoded as PNG for app storage; the original embedded EXIF metadata is not retained. The image itself can still contain private or identifiable details. Your original photo outside BurnPair is not changed.

Exports and backups

You choose whether to export CSV/PDF reports or a complete backup, and where to save or share them. A complete backup can include your records and photos. Files you save to iCloud Drive, another storage provider or another app are handled under that provider’s settings and privacy terms. BurnPair does not control those external copies.

Restoring a backup adds recovered groups with new identifiers instead of replacing existing groups. A restored backup can contain more than two groups; those records remain viewable, editable and exportable without Pro, while creating or duplicating further groups requires Pro. Delete records using the app’s controls. The app removes local photos no longer referenced by records. Temporary export files are cleared after sharing and again at the next app launch as a fallback. Deleting something in BurnPair does not delete copies you previously exported or shared. Removing the app removes its local app data; device backups and files outside the app may remain under your system or provider settings. Keep a backup you control if you need to move devices.

One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple. BurnPair uses Apple’s purchase services to check or restore the Pro entitlement. We do not receive your payment card details, and the app does not send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server. Apple may process Apple Account and transaction information under its own privacy policy. Pro unlocks creation of more new experiment groups (up to 100 groups in total); existing records and exports remain available without Pro.

No advertising or developer analytics

BurnPair includes no advertising, tracking SDK or developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. We do not sell or share your local app records for advertising. Apple may provide its own diagnostics or store reporting according to your Apple settings; this notice does not control Apple’s services.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Avoid sending more personal information than needed. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to records we must retain for legitimate legal or operational reasons.

This support website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information when serving the site under its own privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this notice if BurnPair’s features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current notice.
