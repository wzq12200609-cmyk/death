CueSplice Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026.

CueSplice is an offline rehearsal music editor provided by Jason Wu. It lets you edit your own local audio files on iPhone and iPad.

Audio and projects stay on your device

Imported audio is copied into the app’s local storage. Project names, clip settings, waveform data, language preference and the local free-project counter are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this information or your audio.

No account, advertising or analytics

The app has no developer account system, advertisements, tracking SDK or analytics service. We do not collect personal data through the app.

Apple In-App Purchases

An optional one-time purchase unlocks unlimited new projects. Apple processes payment and purchase restoration. The app uses StoreKit to check the verified purchase entitlement on your device; we do not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s own services and privacy terms apply to its processing.

Files you choose to share

Exports and complete project backups are sent only to the destination you select in the system share sheet. A full project backup includes the imported source audio and project information. The selected app, service or recipient may process those files under its own privacy terms.

Device backups and deletion

Local app data may be included in backups managed by your device and Apple settings. Removing the app can remove its local data. Copies you exported or shared remain at their selected destinations. Keep full project backups of important work.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Please avoid sending private audio unless needed for a support request and you choose to share it. Contact wzq12200609@gmail.com.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s data handling changes.
